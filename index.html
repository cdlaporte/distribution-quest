How to Play

W A S DMove ← ↑ ↓ → or hold the mouseShoot SpaceDash (you can't be hurt while dashing) EOpen chests, use shrines, shop, talk, take the stairs P / EscPause  ·  M mute
  • Clear rooms to open the doors. Each floor has a boss guarding the stairs. There are 5 floors, one for each section of Unit 3.
  • 🔒 Stat Chests are locked with a stats problem. Solve it and pick a power-up.
  • ⛲ Shrines of the Mean heal you when you solve their problem.
  • 🔮 Boss shields break when you solve the glowing rune.
  • ⭐ Perfect solve = first try, no hints, for a better reward. Wrong answers never hurt you. After 2 misses you can see the full solution.
  • Keep scratch paper and a calculator handy. For 3.4, keep Desmos open in another tab.
  • The game saves at the start of every floor. Press Continue next class to pick up where you left off.
